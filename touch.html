User Manual

Getting Started

Hand Controller uses your webcam to track hand gestures in real-time, enabling touchless control of your cursor and virtual keyboard.

Launching

Click the LAUNCH button to start. A camera picker will appear so you can select your preferred input device. The status dot turns green when active.

Mouse Control

Move your hand in front of the camera to control the cursor. Adjust Sensitivity, Smoothness, and Dead Zone in the Mouse tab to fine-tune feel.

Virtual Keyboard

Bring fingertips close together to simulate a tap. Tap Sensitivity controls how close fingers must be, while Tap Cooldown prevents accidental repeat taps.

Overlay Options

  • Hand Skeleton — shows tracking lines over your hand
  • Live Selfie — a small camera preview at a corner of your screen
  • Gesture Command — displays the recognized gesture as text

Tips

For best results, ensure your hand is well-lit and the background is contrasting. Keep your hand within the camera frame and use deliberate gestures.
